## Intent

A 15-second, unnarrated motion graphic explaining how electricity flows through
a simple circuit: a battery pushes current out through wires, around a loop,
through a light bulb (the load), and back to the battery. Clean schematic
diagram, current visualized as glowing dots traveling the loop, bulb lights up
once current reaches it. Short on-screen labels carry the explanation instead
of narration.

## Notes

- Single scene, no narration/voiceover, no user-supplied assets.
- Style: dark technical/educational look — navy background, cyan wire/current
  accent, warm amber bulb glow. Oswald (display) + IBM Plex Mono (labels).
- Routed to `/general-video`: the circuit-diagram-with-flowing-current motif
  isn't covered by any installed `/motion-graphics` category (no diagram/
  flowchart block is actually installed despite being mentioned in its
  catalog map), so this is a hand-authored freeform composition instead.
